## Overview

Mem uses AI to automatically tag and connect notes, allowing users to gather information without spending time organizing it manually. The AI keeps everything connected and organized for easy retrieval.

## Features

- AI-powered automatic tagging of notes
- Semantic search across all stored content
- Topic-based browsing through automatic tags
- Ability to gather bits of information and trust the AI to organize them
- Integration capabilities with Zapier for workflow automation
- Email-to-mem conversion with automatic tagging
- Team summary sharing via Slack
