## Overview

Beebole is an employee timesheet system designed for medium-sized companies and teams. It enables organizations to track time spent by employees and contractors while controlling project costs.

## Features

- **Timesheet Tracking**: Track time spent by employees and contractors
- **Project Cost Control**: Monitor and control project costs across teams
- **Customizable Reporting**: Generate reports tailored to organizational needs
- **KPI Dashboards**: Easy drag-and-drop feature to create KPI tracking systems
- **Multi-Currency Support**: Supports multiple currencies for international teams
- **Multi-Language**: Application available in 8 languages
- **Global Team Support**: Designed for international workforces distributed around the world

## Pricing

$6.99 per user per month.

## Target Users

International teams with a globally distributed workforce who need to track time and meet company goals.
