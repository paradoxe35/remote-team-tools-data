## Overview

HubSpot Task Management is part of the broader HubSpot CRM platform, designed to help sales, marketing, and customer success teams manage follow-ups and day-to-day activities tied directly to customer data. Rather than a standalone project management system, it focuses on task execution within revenue-driven workflows.

## Features

- Tasks connected to contacts, deals, tickets, and campaigns
- Task creation from emails, CRM records, or automation rules
- Centralized task queue for tracking and prioritization
- Dashboards showing task completion, deal progress, and campaign performance
- Automation rules for task creation and assignment
- Alignment of execution with business goals
- Sales pipeline and marketing initiative visibility

## Platform Support

- Web-based
- iOS
- Android

## Ideal For

Sales, marketing, and customer success teams managing tasks tied to CRM workflows, who need visibility into who is doing what across sales pipelines or marketing initiatives.

## Limitations

Lacks native Gantt charts, dependency management, resource planning, and portfolio-level controls. Best used as a supporting tool alongside a dedicated project management platform for delivery-focused projects.

## Pricing

Available as part of HubSpot's CRM platform with free and paid tiers.
