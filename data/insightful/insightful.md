## Overview

Insightful (formerly Workpuls) is a flexible employee monitoring platform offering real-time visibility into team activities. It supports two deployment modes: automatic tracking for teams on company computers and a clock in/out system for remote workers on personal devices, balancing oversight with employee trust.

## Features

- Real-time monitoring and productivity tracking
- App usage analytics and website monitoring
- Two deployment modes: automatic tracking (company computers) and clock in/out (personal devices)
- Custom reports with clear visuals to identify productivity trends and workflow issues
- Manual time entry and activity alerts
- Historical data access
- Role-based labeling of apps and websites as productive or non-productive
- Simple, clean user interface requiring minimal training

## Integrations

- HR and productivity tools
