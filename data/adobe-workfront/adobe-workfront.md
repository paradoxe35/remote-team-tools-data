## Overview

Adobe Workfront is an enterprise work management platform designed for large teams who need to manage complex workflows, coordinate cross-functional projects, and gain real-time visibility into work across departments. It is particularly suited for large enterprises and marketing operations teams managing high volumes of work.

## Features

- **Blueprints (Customizable Project Templates)**: Ready-to-deploy templates for common use cases like digital asset production, IT organization setup, and services delivery plans. Templates can be adjusted and saved as reusable starter projects.
- **Automated Workflow Templates**: Lock in review stages, approval sequences, and stakeholder permissions so the same process runs consistently every time.
- **Resource Capacity Planning**: Track team utilization and forecast staffing needs to balance workloads across projects and individuals.
- **Digital Proofing**: Review, annotate, and approve creative assets directly within Workfront using built-in proofing tools.
- **Custom Dashboards and Reporting**: Build visual dashboards with configurable report widgets and export data to BI tools or data lakes.
- **Work Request Management**: Create a centralized intake channel with custom forms that automatically route requests to the right team.
- Configurable workflows, task dependencies, and approval stages.
- Compliance checks and stakeholder sign-off management.
- Resource and budget visibility across initiatives.

## Integrations

Integrations include Adobe Experience Manager Assets, Adobe Creative Cloud, Adobe Express, Frame.io, GenStudio for Performance Marketing, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Figma, Marketo Engage, and Acrobat Sign.
