## Overview

Nextiva consolidates voice, video, team chat, SMS, and contact-center capabilities into a single platform, reducing app switching and enabling remote teams to manage customer conversations in one place. The platform strives for 99.999% uptime and offers built-in contact center features with AI call summaries and transcription.

## Features

- HD voice, video meetings, screen sharing, and team messaging
- Call routing, interactive voice response, call recording, and analytics
- Integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other major CRM platforms
- Mobile and desktop apps that support working from anywhere
- AI-powered transcription, call summaries, and real-time dashboards (available on higher plans)
- Built-in contact center capabilities with omnichannel routing

## Pricing

- Core plan: $15 per user per month
- Engage and Power Suite CX plans available for advanced routing and AI features
