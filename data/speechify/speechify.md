## Overview

Speechify is primarily marketed as a reading assistant that converts written content into audio, allowing users to listen to articles, emails, marketing plans, and documents while multitasking.

## Features

- Celebrity voice options, including Snoop Dogg and Gwyneth Paltrow for reading content aloud
- Speechify Studio for generating and downloading voices for personal projects
- Customizable voice settings: speed, pitch, and volume controls
- Pronunciation fine-tuning and pause insertion for precise timing
- Automatic narration-to-visuals sync for slide-based video creation
- Background music integration and export functionality

## Pricing

Free and paid tiers available. Specific pricing details vary by plan.
