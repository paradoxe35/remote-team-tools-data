## Overview

Beekeeper is a communication and collaboration platform designed specifically for frontline and deskless workforces. It enables organizations to connect non-desk employees with company communications, scheduling, task management, and peer-to-peer messaging through a mobile-first approach.

## Key Features

- Mobile-first communication platform
- Company news and announcements feed
- Task management and assignment for frontline workers
- Shift scheduling and management
- Peer-to-peer messaging and group chats
- Document and policy sharing
- Surveys and feedback collection
- Real-time translations for multilingual teams

## Integrations

Integrates with major HR systems and enterprise platforms via APIs and pre-built connectors.
