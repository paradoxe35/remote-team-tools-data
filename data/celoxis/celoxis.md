## Overview

Celoxis is a comprehensive project and portfolio management platform designed for mid-size to large teams that need a centralized platform to manage multiple projects, resources, and stakeholders at once. It consolidates project planning, resource management, financial tracking, risk management, and executive reporting into a single system.

## Features

- **Interactive Gantt Chart**: Automatically adjusts to changes in real time; schedules update without manual rework when dependencies shift.
- **Project Accounting**: Track budget spend, revenue forecasting, and profit margins across projects and portfolios in real time.
- **Resource Capacity Planning**: View team availability, assign work based on skills, and receive instant alerts when someone is overloaded.
- **Portfolio Management Dashboard**: Monitor progress, budgets, and risks across multiple projects in a single customizable view.
- **Built-in Workflow Apps**: Track risks, issues, change requests, and bugs alongside the project itself without juggling separate spreadsheets.
- **Custom Routing Rules and Escalation Policies**: Keep the right people informed at the right time without extra setup.
- **Client Portal**: Share project updates, files, and discussions with clients through a free, customizable portal built into the platform.
- Support for Agile, Waterfall, and hybrid delivery methodologies.
- Both cloud and on-premise deployment options.

## Integrations

Integrations include Microsoft Project, Microsoft Excel, Google Drive, Outlook, Salesforce, Jira, QuickBooks Online, Slack, Trello, and Okta.

Plans start from $10/user/month (billed annually). A 14-day free trial and free demo are available.
