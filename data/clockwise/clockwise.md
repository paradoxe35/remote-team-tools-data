## Overview

Clockwise is designed for teams and promises to save users one hour per week through automated calendar optimization. It schedules a one-hour break shortly after you start using it to demonstrate its value.

## Features

- AI-powered calendar optimization for teams
- Automatic scheduling of work, breaks, and meetings for harmony
- Proven time savings (averages one hour per week)
- Customizable settings to create an ideal day
- Calendar rearrangement to protect important time blocks
- Team availability coordination

## Pricing

Free and paid tiers available.
