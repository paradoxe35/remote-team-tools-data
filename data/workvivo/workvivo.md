## Overview

Workvivo is a smart alternative to legacy intranets, designed to boost inclusivity and engagement while keeping employees informed via a social media-like experience. It enables remote teams to post content, get updates, connect, share, and interact in real-time via livestream, multimedia, and personal activity feeds.

## Features

- Social media-style activity feeds for company updates and employee posts
- Livestream capabilities for real-time communication and events
- Peer recognition with employee nominations, awards, and shout-outs
- Polls and surveys for managers to collect valuable employee feedback and monitor morale
- Multimedia content sharing for rich internal communications
- Email-reducing design to keep everyone informed without inbox overload

## Use Cases

- Company-wide internal communications and news sharing
- Employee recognition and engagement programs
- Collecting feedback through polls and surveys
- Reducing email clutter with feed-based updates

## Pricing

- Two plans available: one for 250-2,000 employees and one for more than 2,000 employees
- Add-ons available for team chat and employee insights
- Requires custom quotes; no standard pricing published
