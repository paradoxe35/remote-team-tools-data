## Overview

ProofHub is a project management and team collaboration platform designed for organizations that need strong control over reviews, approvals, and internal communication. It brings task management, file proofing, and team discussions together in a single workspace.

## Features

- Multiple project views: task lists, Kanban boards, Gantt charts, and calendars
- Built-in proofing for documents, designs, and files with inline comments
- Stakeholder review, change requests, and approval workflows
- Discussions, announcements, and project-level chats
- Task assignment and progress tracking
- Calendar-based scheduling
- Centralized communication reducing reliance on external tools and email threads
- Flat-rate pricing model

## Platform Support

- Web-based
- iOS
- Android

## Ideal For

Creative teams, agencies, and marketing departments that need structured task management with built-in review and approval workflows.

## Pricing

ProofHub offers flat-rate pricing with no per-user fees, making costs predictable for growing teams.
