## Overview

Frame.io is a platform designed for creative teams to collaborate on video content, offering tools for media review and feedback. It streamlines the feedback process on video projects with real-time collaboration tools that cater specifically to video editors and producers.

## Standout Features

- Real-time video reviews with synchronized playback
- Time-stamped comments for precise feedback on specific video frames
- Frame-accurate drawing and annotation tools
- Version comparison to track changes between edits
- Secure media sharing with customizable access controls
- Approval workflows for sign-off processes
- Media asset management and organization

## Integrations

Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, Slack, Trello, Asana, Microsoft Teams, Zapier, Dropbox
