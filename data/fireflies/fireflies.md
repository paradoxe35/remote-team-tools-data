## Overview

Fireflies transcribes meetings and tracks conversation topics. It uses an AI bot called Fred that handles summarizing meeting contents, generating text, and searching through meeting history.

## Features

- Automatic meeting transcription
- Conversation topic tracking
- AI bot (Fred) for summarizing meeting contents
- Text generation from meeting content
- Searchable meeting history
- AI-powered extraction of key action items
- Integration capabilities with Zapier for downstream workflow automation
- CRM update capabilities from meeting transcripts
- Project management task creation from meeting data

Free and paid tiers available.
