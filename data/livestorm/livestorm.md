## Overview

Livestorm is a video engagement platform designed for both live and asynchronous communications. It supports hosting internal and external meetings, recording content, and making it available on demand.

## Features

- Automatic recordings for creating asynchronous video resources like on-demand training, deminars, and walkthroughs
- Events automation to set pre-recorded events (e.g., webinars) to start and end at specific times, imitating live events without intervention
- Project management and collaboration via integrations with Slack, Miro, and Zapier for automated workflows
- Quick scheduling using integrations with Google Calendar, Calendly, and Outlook
- Email automation for sharing recordings, sending reminders, and async follow-ups

## Use Cases

- Onboarding remote employees
- Internal training and development
- Webinars and product demos
- Customer success events
