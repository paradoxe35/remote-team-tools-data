## Overview

TeamGantt is a project management tool designed for teams that plan and manage work primarily through timelines. Its core strength lies in clean, visual Gantt charts that make it easy to map tasks, dependencies, milestones, and deadlines.

## Features

- Visual Gantt charts with drag-and-drop timeline controls
- Automatic dependency updates when timelines shift
- Task dependencies and milestone tracking
- Basic task collaboration: comments, file attachments, and assignees
- Workload views to see who is over- or under-allocated
- Schedule balancing for small teams
- Intuitive, accessible project planning

## Platform Support

- Web-based
- iOS
- Android

## Ideal For

Teams that rely heavily on Gantt charts for project planning and timeline visibility, valuing clarity and simplicity in scheduling over deep workflows, automation, or financial management.
