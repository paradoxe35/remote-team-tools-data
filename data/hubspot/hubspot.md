## Overview

HubSpot is a comprehensive CRM and marketing automation platform that helps distributed teams streamline workflows, manage leads, and automate sales and marketing processes. It supports asynchronous workflows by triggering notifications to sales teams when leads are nurtured and ready for conversion.

## Features

- CRM platform for managing contacts, deals, and company information
- Marketing hub with email automation, campaign tracking, and lead scoring
- Sales pipeline management with deal stages and automated task creation
- Workflow automation to connect marketing, sales, and service processes
- Lead capture forms and integration with website visitors tracking
- Custom notifications to alert team members when leads are sales-ready
- Integration with Zapier, Gmail, Slack, and 1000+ other apps

## Use Cases

- Automated lead capture and nurturing workflows
- Adding new leads from online forms into CRM systems
- Notifying sales teams when leads are ready for follow-up
- Customer onboarding and engagement automation

- Free CRM plan with basic contact management and pipeline tools

- Sales Hub: starts at $15 per user per month
