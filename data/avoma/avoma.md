## Overview

Avoma focuses on the context around meetings beyond just transcription. It tracks speaking stats, filler word counts, monologues, and talking-to-listening ratios, helping teams understand pressing issues and improve performance.

## Features

- Meeting transcription as a foundation
- Speaking stats tracking: filler word count, monologues, talking-to-listening ratios
- Call topic recording to identify pressing issues
- AI scoring tools for sales team improvement
- AI-powered transcript analysis for next steps
- Automated generation of tailored follow-up emails
- Slack notifications for product and sales teams when insights align with strategic priorities
- Zapier integration for AI-orchestrated workflows
