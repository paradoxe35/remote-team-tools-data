## Overview

NordVPN encrypts internet traffic, making it suitable for remote workers who connect from home offices and public spaces. It adds a security layer for distributed teams accessing company resources over untrusted networks.

## Features

- Strong encryption for all internet traffic
- Kill switch to prevent data exposure if connection drops
- Global server network for reliable access
- No-logs privacy posture
- Split tunneling for selective traffic routing

## Pricing

- Consumer plans range from $4.99 to $8.99 per user per month
- Business plans are available separately
