## Overview

Buffer is a social media management productivity app designed for teams and content managers who want to manage all their social media accounts through a single interface. It enables content scheduling, multi-account management, and provides data analytics and insights to help teams refine their social media strategy and improve SEO performance on social platforms.

## Features

- Two-step account login for enhanced security
- Post scheduling across multiple social media platforms
- Unified management of multiple social media accounts
- Data analytics and insights for performance tracking
- RSS feed connectivity for automated content discovery
- Content sharing and connection across all social media apps

## Pros

- Two-step account login for better security
- Scheduling capabilities for posts and tweets
- Multiple social media accounts management from one dashboard
- Data analytics and insights to guide content strategy
- RSS feeds connectivity for content discovery
