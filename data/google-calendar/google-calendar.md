## Overview

Google Calendar is a cloud-based scheduling and calendar management tool from Google. It enables remote teams to set reminders, share availability, schedule meetings across time zones, and coordinate with other Google Workspace tools.

## Features

- Multiple calendar sharing to make availability visible to the team
- Time zone support for scheduling across distributed teams globally
- Meeting scheduling with auto-suggested times based on availability
- Integration with Gmail, Google Meet, Google Drive, and other Google Workspace apps
- Smart reminders and notifications for events and tasks
- Color-coding and categories for organizing different types of events

## Use Cases

- Setting reminders for deadlines and tasks
- Sharing availability with remote team members
- Scheduling meetings at times that work across multiple time zones
- Coordinating team events and company-wide updates

## Pricing

- Free for personal use with a Google Account
- Included with Google Workspace business plans starting at $6 per user per month
