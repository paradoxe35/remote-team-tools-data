## Overview

DeskFlex is a workspace management tool built for hybrid and remote teams. It helps organizations manage office space efficiently by providing desk booking, scheduling, and hot-desking capabilities.

## Key Features

- Desk booking and scheduling for flexible office arrangements
- Workspace utilization tracking and analytics
- Support for hot-desking and assigned seating models
- Calendar integration for seamless scheduling
- Customizable workspace layouts and floor plans
- Support for hybrid team operations

## Ideal Use Cases

- Hybrid teams that split time between office and remote work
- Organizations implementing hot-desking policies
- Companies optimizing office space usage post-pandemic
