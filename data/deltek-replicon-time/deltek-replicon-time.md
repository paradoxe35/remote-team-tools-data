## Overview

Deltek Replicon Time is a project time tracking platform for professional services teams and organizations that need precise project costing, resource allocation, and real-time visibility into project hours and budgets. It is well-suited for consulting, IT services, and staffing agencies that bill clients by the hour.

## Features

- **ZeroTime™ Automatic Time Capture**: Automatically pulls time data from 100+ work apps (e.g., Jira, Slack, GitHub) so teams don't need to manually reconstruct hours.
- **AI-Powered Timesheet Validation**: Flags anomalies and missing entries before they become billing errors.
- **Estimated vs. Actual Cost Comparison**: Provides a live read on budget burn without exporting data to a separate tool.
- **Global Labor Compliance Tracking**: Monitor compliance with labor laws across 145+ jurisdictions in 75+ countries, covering pay, overtime, breaks, and leave rules.
- **CloudClock Time Capture**: Record time and attendance via a dedicated cloud-based time clock device, alongside web and mobile options.
- **Custom Reports and Dashboards**: Build tailored reports and connect to Tableau and Power BI for project time, costs, and workforce data.
- **Expense Tracking**: Log and manage project-related expenses alongside time data for accurate billing and reimbursement.

## Integrations

Integrations include SAP, Oracle, ADP, QuickBooks, Microsoft Dynamics, Salesforce, Jira, Slack, Tableau, and Power BI.
