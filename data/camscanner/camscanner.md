## Overview

CamScanner is a mobile document scanning application that transforms smartphones into portable scanners. Users can capture physical documents with their phone camera, and the app automatically enhances image quality and background. Scanned documents can be edited on-device and exported in multiple formats, with OCR functionality enabling text extraction from scanned images.

## Features

- Automatic image quality and background enhancement for scanned documents
- OCR (Optical Character Recognition) for editing and searching PDF content
- Synchronization across multiple devices
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Wireless printing support
- Global faxing support
- Multiple image edit features for scanned documents
- Export files in multiple formats
