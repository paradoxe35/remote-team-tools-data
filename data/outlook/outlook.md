## Overview

Outlook is Microsoft's email and personal information management platform used for asynchronous communication. It combines email, calendar, contacts, and task management capabilities to help remote teams stay organized and productive across time zones.

## Features

- Email with threaded conversations, focused inbox, and categorization
- Integrated calendar for scheduling and sharing availability
- Task management with assignment and tracking capabilities
- Deep integration with Microsoft 365 apps (Word, Excel, Teams, OneDrive)
- Focused inbox to prioritize important messages
- Shared mailboxes and calendars for team coordination

## Use Cases

- Professional email communication with internal and external stakeholders
- Scheduling meetings across time zones
- Managing team calendars and shared availability
- Task tracking and project coordination

## Pricing

- Included with Microsoft 365 subscriptions starting at $6.99 per user per month for Business Basic
- Higher tiers available with additional desktop apps and security features
