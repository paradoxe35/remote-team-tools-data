## Overview

Mailchimp is primarily known as a marketing tool, but it is also widely used for internal communications within remote teams. It makes it easy for busy teams to catch up on company news and important announcements through regular newsletters delivered to their inbox.

## Features

- Intuitive UI with drag-and-drop email builder for creating branded newsletters
- Customizable email templates with eye-catching visuals
- List segmentation to target specific teams or departments
- Open and engagement tracking to measure what content resonates
- Automated email scheduling for regular internal newsletters
- Analytics dashboard to monitor campaign performance

## Use Cases

- Internal company newsletters for distributed teams
- Announcements and important organizational updates
- Employee onboarding communication series
- Department-specific updates and news

## Pricing

- Free plan: up to 500 contacts and 1,000 emails per month
- Essentials plan: starts at $13 per month for 500 contacts and 5,000 emails per month
- Standard and Premium tiers available with advanced features and higher limits
