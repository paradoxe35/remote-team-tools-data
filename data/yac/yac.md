## Overview

Yac is a voice messaging tool that allows teams to communicate asynchronously through voice notes, enabling quick, clear communication without the need for real-time meetings. What makes Yac stand out is its ability to convey tone and context through voice, making it especially useful for teams that need to avoid the pitfalls of miscommunication in text.

## Standout Features

- Voice messaging for asynchronous communication
- Screen recording with voiceover capability
- Voice notes that can be replayed at any time
- Transcription of voice messages for accessibility
- Organized channels and threads for team discussions
- Quick recording and sharing without scheduling meetings
- Reduces the need for synchronous meetings

## Integrations

Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zapier, Trello, Notion, Asana, Google Workspace, Jira, ClickUp, Discord
