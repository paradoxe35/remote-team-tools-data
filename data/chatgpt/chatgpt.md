## Overview

ChatGPT is the current industry-leading AI chatbot, serving as the first conversational AI to achieve widespread adoption. It handles conversational queries, content creation, research, and image generation through a unified interface.

## Features

- **Conversational AI**: Ask questions and get answers in a natural conversational style
- **Code Writing**: Generates and debugs code across multiple programming languages
- **Mathematical Calculations**: Performs complex mathematical reasoning and calculations
- **Image Generation**: Natively generates images using autoregressive generation for increased accuracy and control
- **Content Creation**: Write marketing copy, conduct market research, and draft sales emails
- **Feedback Handling**: Accepts natural language feedback to refine and tweak generated outputs
- **Advanced Reasoning**: Uses advanced reasoning and logic for complex tasks
- **Zapier Integration**: Connect with thousands of other apps to orchestrate workflows

## Use Cases

- Summarizing survey responses into key themes
- Generating personalized follow-up emails based on participant engagement
- Automating image creation from form responses or CRM data
- Writing marketing copy and conducting market research

## Pricing

Free tier available with GPT-4o access. ChatGPT Plus subscription unlocks additional capabilities including o3-mini, GPT-4o, o1 models, and image generation features.
