## Overview

Everhour is a time tracking tool known for its simplicity and effectiveness for both employees and managers. It streamlines tracking time for projects and billable hours while integrating with popular project management and task management platforms.

## Features

- **Easy Clock In/Out**: Simple interface for employees to clock in and out
- **Direct Tool Integration**: Track time directly from tools already in use — Asana, Basecamp, Jira, GitHub, Monday.com, Azure DevOps, and more — without switching between apps
- **Timesheet Approval**: Managers can easily approve time submissions
- **Real-Time Progress Tracking**: Monitor project progress in real time
- **Budget Monitoring**: Track project budgets and receive notifications when limits are approached
- **Detailed Reporting**: Comprehensive reports to ensure projects stay on track and within budget
- **Billable Hours Tracking**: Straightforward tracking of billable hours for client invoicing

## Pricing

- Free plan: Available (without integrations)
- Team plans: Starting at $8 per user per month

## Target Users

Agencies managing multiple clients who need to track billable hours for each project and ensure accurate invoicing.
