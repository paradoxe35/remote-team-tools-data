## Overview

Slite is a knowledge base tool that enables remote teams to create, organize, and share documentation in a centralized wiki. It provides an intuitive platform for teams to build and maintain company knowledge, document workflows, and ensure everyone has access to the information they need.

## Features

- Collaborative document editing with real-time updates
- Organized document structure with channels and categories
- Search functionality to quickly find relevant documentation
- Templates for common document types like SOPs, meeting notes, and onboarding guides
- Integrations with Slack, Google Drive, and other popular tools
- Visitor analytics to understand documentation usage

## Use Cases

- Building and maintaining company wikis
- Documenting workflows, processes, and SOPs
- Creating onboarding resources for new employees
- Storing and sharing team knowledge across departments

## Pricing

- Free plan available with limited features
- Paid plans include unlimited documents, advanced search, and integrations
- Custom pricing available for larger teams
