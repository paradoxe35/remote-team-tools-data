## Overview

Clarizen is a project and portfolio management platform designed for organizations that need strong alignment between strategic goals and execution. It helps leadership teams, PMOs, and enterprise project managers track initiatives from high-level objectives down to day-to-day work.

## Features

- Manage projects, programs, and portfolios in a single system
- Real-time dashboards showing progress, risks, and dependencies
- Structured workflows, approvals, and role-based access controls
- Resource capacity planning and utilization tracking
- Financial oversight including cost impact analysis across initiatives
- Reporting and analytics geared toward executive decision-making
- Compliance support for organizations with formal project controls

## Platform Support

Web, iOS, Android

## Ideal For

Enterprises and PMOs that need strong governance, portfolio oversight, and alignment between strategy and execution.

## Limitations

- Setup and configuration often require dedicated administration
- Everyday task management may feel heavier compared to more modern, team-centric tools
- Less suited for teams prioritizing flexibility, faster onboarding, and hands-on project delivery
